# Section 2 & 3: Aims, Objectives & Scope

## 2.1 Project Aims
SavePlate aims to reduce household and community food waste by offering real-time inventory tracking, expiration alerts, meal planning, and community food sharing.

## 2.2 Key Objectives
- Provide intuitive food item tracking with automated shelf-life warnings.
- Offer smart meal suggestions based on ingredients approaching expiration.
- Enable peer-to-peer donation and community sharing for surplus food.
- Measure environmental impact through waste reduction analytics.
